# Brand Identity — davidmgray.com

> Internal brand guide for David Gray's personal website. Single source of truth for positioning, voice, and visual direction. Written from scratch to support a deliberate repositioning from embedded/systems engineering toward cloud and AI infrastructure. Living document.

---

## 0. The repositioning (read this first)

David is a **cloud / platform engineer with deep systems roots, building toward ML-platform / AI-infrastructure work.** Three facts anchor everything:

1. **Today (true now):** Product security engineer on a cloud infrastructure team, building cloud infrastructure for enterprise applications. Strong, current, real.
2. **Background (the depth):** Years of embedded / real-time / defense systems engineering (C/C++/Go/Ada, kernels, RTOS, anti-tamper). This is the "I understand the machine all the way down, I am not just a Python scripter" credential.
3. **Direction (where it's going):** AI was David's first love in college (computer vision, ML projects). The trajectory is toward **ML platform / ML infrastructure engineering** — the layer where cloud infra meets AI workloads (model serving, GPU orchestration, MLOps/LLMOps done right). An MS in CS (cloud, networking, distributed systems) backs the move.

**The honesty rule — non-negotiable.** The site states the AI direction as a *direction*, never as a current credential. David does **not** build AI infrastructure at work today; he builds cloud infrastructure for enterprise apps, on a path toward AI. Technical hiring managers detect overclaiming in seconds and it sinks a repositioning. We never write "I build the infrastructure AI runs on" in the present tense. We write the trajectory honestly and let the proof (projects, writing) earn the AI half.

**Why this works:** "Systems engineer who understands infrastructure down to the metal, doing real cloud work now, deliberately moving into AI infrastructure, with strong opinions about how LLMOps/MLOps *should* be built" is a credible, distinctive, and completely truthful story. It doesn't require pretending to be anything.

## 1. Positioning

**Headline:** *From the metal to the model.*
(Directional by nature — it describes a journey, embedded → cloud → AI, not a current job title. That's exactly why it's honest.)

**One-liner:** *I build cloud infrastructure for a living, I came up close to the metal, and I'm building toward the platforms AI runs on.*

**Positioning statement:**
For hiring managers on cloud and AI-infrastructure teams, David Gray is a platform engineer with rare end-to-end depth — from embedded kernels to enterprise cloud — who is deliberately and visibly moving into ML-platform / AI-infrastructure work. Unlike candidates who arrive at AI infra as either pure ML people (no systems depth) or pure infra people (no ML instinct), David sits on the seam: real cloud-infra experience, genuine close-to-metal systems understanding, an early love of AI, and a documented point of view on how AI infrastructure should be built.

**Target role (the headline identity):** ML Platform / ML Infrastructure Engineer.
**Current honest framing:** Cloud / platform engineer (product security, cloud infra team), building toward ML platform work.

**Audience priority:**

1. **Hiring managers on cloud / AI-infra teams** — the conversion target. They must, within ~30 seconds, see (a) real cloud/systems credibility, (b) the AI-infra direction stated honestly, (c) proof, (d) a way to reach David.
2. **Technical peers** — served by the same content. Good writing and real projects earn respect and shares, which feeds back into hiring.

## 2. The throughline (how the past serves the future)

Embedded is **the foundation, framed as an edge — never as "all I know."**

- It proves David understands systems down to the metal: scheduling, latency, memory, concurrency, security. These are *exactly* the intuitions that make AI-infra work good (GPU scheduling, inference latency, resource constraints, secure model serving).
- The story is **return, not pivot.** AI was first; systems made David rigorous; cloud is the current craft; AI infrastructure is where they converge. Not "starting over in AI" — "bringing systems discipline to AI."
- We never let embedded read as the ceiling. It's the basement that makes the upper floors trustworthy.

**Stack-up narrative (use everywhere):** metal → systems → cloud → AI infrastructure. Each layer earns the next.

- **Sharp, not abrasive.** Has opinions, names what's broken, backs every take with experience. A practitioner with standards.
- **Honest, not modest-to-a-fault.** States exactly where the skills are (cloud, systems) and where the ambition points (AI infra) without inflating either.

**Voice in one line:** a systems engineer with real opinions about infrastructure, genuinely excited about the AI layer, who refuses to bullshit about what he has and hasn't done yet.

## 4. Voice & tone

**Voice: sharp & opinionated, from authority.** First person, direct, technically specific. Has takes and defends them with experience. Plain words, concrete numbers, no hype.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Most MLOps tooling hides the systems problems instead of solving them. I've spent years in those systems." | "Passionate about leveraging cutting-edge AI/ML solutions." |
| "I build cloud infrastructure for enterprise apps. I'm building toward the AI-infra version of that." | "I build the infrastructure AI runs on." (overclaim — false today) |
| "Came up writing kernels and schedulers. That's why I think about inference latency the way I do." | Hiding the embedded past, or treating AI as brand-new. |
| Active, specific verbs: built, shipped, reduced, reverse-engineered, hardened. | Passive corporate filler; "synergy," "leverage," "results-driven." |

**The transition, in David's own words (template):**
> "Cloud infrastructure is my craft today. ML platform work is where I'm taking it — and I'm documenting how I think it should be built along the way."

State the direction confidently; never apologize for not being there yet; never claim to be there already.

**Tone by surface:**
- **Hero / tagline:** confident, memorable. The one place to be bold.
- **About / experience:** factual, metric-led, honest about current vs. direction.
- **Blog:** sharp and didactic — one strong, defensible idea per post. This is where the POV lives.
- **Hire / contact:** warm, explicit, low-friction.
- **404 / microcopy:** playful. Personality lives here.

**Banned words:** passionate, synergy, leverage (verb), ninja/rockstar/guru, results-driven, detail-oriented, "cutting-edge." Curse-free.

## 5. Documenting the transition (editorial stance)

David is documenting the move from systems → cloud → AI infrastructure **openly, but always from authority.** This is a strategic asset, handled correctly:

- **Frame as POV, not catch-up.** Not "I'm learning AI." Instead: "I'm a systems engineer who's noticed LLMOps/MLOps is often done badly, and here's how it should be built by someone who understands what's underneath." Same activity, opposite signal.
- **Earn the AI half in public.** Every honest post about cloud-for-AI, model serving, GPU/inference systems, or "what good ML infra looks like" converts the trajectory into evidence.
- **Never apologize.** No "I'm new to this," no hedging. Curiosity expressed as a practitioner forming opinions, not a beginner asking permission.
- This serves both audiences: peers respect a clear POV; hiring managers hire people who already think like the role.

## 6. Visual direction — Polished, with signature moments

Credible and fast by default; interactive craft concentrated in a few earned places.

### Principles
1. **Calm chrome, lively focal points.** Restrained near-monochrome frame; color and motion concentrate in the hero and in one or two live demos.
2. **Signature moments, not motion everywhere.** The particle field hero and a live infra/ML demo carry the "builds cool things" signal. Everything else stays clean.
3. **Polish reads as competence.** For a hiring-manager audience, restraint and speed signal seniority. The playfulness is a seasoning, not the meal.
4. **Respect the reader.** Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; fully usable with motion off and with JS disabled. Performance budget non-negotiable (§9).
5. **The web is part of the portfolio.** A well-built interactive site is itself evidence of front-to-back capability.

### Signature motif
The **cursor-reactive particle field** (existing `Hero.vue`) is the brand mark in motion — a field of small ellipses that orient toward and scale with the cursor. **Fix it:** replace the current per-frame `Math.random()` RGB (it flickers) with a stable palette-driven scheme sampling the accent gradient by position/cursor distance. Reuse a calmer variant as section dividers / empty-state backdrops.

## 7. Color — Teal primary + Violet accent on near-black

Decided from scratch for this brand (not inherited from the old site). The strategy: **one dominant accent + one complementary**, on a near-black background, dark-first. Rationale and psychology:

- **Near-black, never pure black.** `#0B0F17` (cool-tinted) reads as intentional and expensive, avoids halation/vibration, and makes a single accent glow. Dark is the native environment of this audience (terminals, IDEs, dashboards, AI marketing) — it signals "I live where you live" before a word is read.
- **Teal = primary (infra / trust).** The color of monitoring dashboards and cloud consoles; reads reliable and technical. Lowest-risk credibility signal for hiring managers.
- **Violet = complementary (AI).** The de-facto color of the modern AI wave; reads forward-looking and intelligent. Reserved for AI moments and the gradient end.
- **The teal→violet gradient *is* the brand story in color** — the eye travels from systems to AI exactly as the career does (metal → model). One-plus-one reads senior; three+ accents read junior and dilute the signal.

The implemented values live in [`/static/css/tokens.css`](../static/css/tokens.css) (the single source of truth). Summary of the dark-mode semantic tokens:

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0B0F17` | Page background (near-black, cool tint) |
| `--surface` | `#141A24` | Cards, raised panels |
| `--border` | `#243044` | Hairline dividers (subtle, decorative) |
| `--text` | `#E6EAF2` | Primary text (15.9:1) |
| `--text-muted` | `#94A1B8` | Secondary text (7.3:1) |
| `--accent` | `#5EEAD4` | Primary — links, CTAs, infra energy (13:1) |
| `--accent-2` | `#A78BFA` | Complementary — AI moments, gradient end (7:1) |
| `--gradient-brand` | teal→violet | Hero accent, particle sampling, dividers |

The particle field samples the teal→violet gradient by position/cursor distance — **never random** (kills the old flicker).

**Light mode:** fully defined in `tokens.css` but **off by default**; activates via `prefers-color-scheme: light` or `data-theme="light"`. Accents darken for AA (teal `#0E7C68`, violet `#6D4ED6`; white text on filled accent buttons). Ship dark-only at launch; flip light on later with zero rewrites.

**Accessibility — verified.** Every text/background and text/accent pairing in `tokens.css` meets WCAG 2.1 AA (≥4.5:1 body, ≥3:1 large) in both modes; checked programmatically. Borders are decorative hairlines (no contrast requirement). Re-run the contrast check before changing any accent hex.

## 8. Typography

Keep **Outfit** (already loaded) for display/UI — modern, friendly, fits "polished + a little playful." Add a **monospace** accent for the technical identity inside the polished frame.

- **Display / headings:** Outfit 600/800. Large, tight tracking on the hero.
- **Body / UI:** Outfit 300/400 (pair with **Inter** if Outfit reads thin small).
- **Code / technical accents:** **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono** — inline code, the `DG.` logo mark, project tags, small "systems" labels. The mono is the nod to the metal.

**Type scale (rem, 1.250 major-third):** 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.25 / 1.563 / 1.953 / 2.441 / 3.052. Hero display fluid (clamp). One display face, one body face, one mono. Body line-height 1.6; prose ≤ 70ch.

## 9. Name, mark & motion budget

- **Name treatment:** "David Gray" full name (professional, recruiter-friendly, matches `davidmgray.com`) with **`DG.` in monospace** as the compact logo/favicon mark (continuity with current site).
- **Motion & performance budget:** honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; cap particle count to viewport; pause `requestAnimationFrame` when offscreen / tab hidden; throttle low-end devices. Targets: Lighthouse Perf ≥ 90 mobile, LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1. UI transitions 150–250ms, spring/ease-out; never animate layout (no CLS).
