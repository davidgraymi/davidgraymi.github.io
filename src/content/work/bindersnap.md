My wife complained — repeatedly, correctly — about the policy management
platform at her work. Version control for documents is a solved problem, but the
solution is locked inside developer tooling that nobody outside engineering
would ever agree to use.

Bindersnap is that solution with the developer assumptions removed: painless
all-in-one document management, a real review lifecycle, team collaboration,
search, and an in-browser editor. Branch, review, merge and history, for people
who have never opened a terminal.

## Status: honest version

It is a solo side project and it does not get the love it deserves from me. Grad
school and a full-time job both have opinions about my evenings. The plan is to
eventually hire interns to handle sales and grind down a backlog I will
otherwise never reach.

I am keeping it here because it is the project that best shows how I think about
product: find the group who was told the good tool is not for them, and remove
the reason.
