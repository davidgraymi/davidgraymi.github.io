A word guessing game, written in Dart with Flutter and compiled for the web.

I built it for the reason most small games get built: I wanted to know whether
Flutter's web target was actually usable for something with real interaction and
tight feedback loops. The short answer is yes, with caveats around initial load.

No account, no install, no scoreboard harvesting your email address. Open it and
play.
